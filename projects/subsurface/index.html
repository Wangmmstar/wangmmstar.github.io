The underground is invisible.

GPR can sense buried utilities, but raw radargrams do not directly tell us what is underground, how deep it is, or where it lies in the site.

From radar signals to a spatial map

Sense → Reconstruct → Register

We reconstruct subsurface structure from GPR and use camera-based localization to register detected utilities back into the physical site.

Reconstructing the unseen

The inversion model converts radargrams directly into subsurface maps.

Simulated/augmented validation cases.

Testing on real radargrams
