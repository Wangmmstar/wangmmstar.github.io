Research directions

Where this research could go next.

Our current work establishes a starting point: detect buried utilities and link them to a site. Several bigger questions remain.

01

Beyond locating buried pipes.

Can GPR reveal more than location—such as geometry, material properties, or potential defects? How reliable could these estimates be?

02

Using underground maps to plan work above.

How can buried-utility information be combined with surface conditions and construction plans to support excavation, inspection, and other operations?

03

Can buried utilities become landmarks for robots?

A mobile robot with subsurface sensing could compare observed pipe features with an existing underground map—adding a reference when surface landmarks are unreliable.
