01 / The problem

A Conclusion Doesn’t Reveal the Reasoning.

Identifying where a fire started is only part of the investigation. The deeper challenge is understanding which evidence supports that conclusion—and how competing explanations are examined.

The outcome

A final conclusion.

Where did the fire start?
The process behind it

Evidence. Context. Interpretation.

What was observed? Why did it matter?
Which explanations were reconsidered?

Our approach

An Interactive Environment for Investigative Reasoning.

I developed a Unity-based spatial investigation interface that connects scene exploration, evidence annotation, hypothesis exploration, and structured session recording within a unified workflow.

02 / Immersive exploration

Explore What Remains.

A fire transforms the physical environment, leaving traces of events that can no longer be observed directly. Our interactive environment allows investigators to explore the damaged scene and examine its spatial context.

03 / Spatial investigation

Turn Observations Into Evidence.

Investigators identify and annotate spatial evidence directly within the reconstructed scene, capturing observations, interpretations, and confidence as part of the investigation.

Observe → Annotate → Interpret

04 / Hypothesis exploration

Test Explanations Against Fire Development.

Fire simulations can be explored alongside spatial evidence, allowing investigators to examine candidate explanations and revisit their interpretations.

05 / Observable reasoning

Make the Investigation Process Visible.

The platform captures spatial annotations, investigation decisions, hypothesis revisions, and supporting explanations—creating structured records of how an investigation unfolds.

From a final answer to a traceable investigation process.

Future research

Toward Reasoning-Aware AI Assistance.

Structured investigation traces provide a foundation for studying how investigators reason, where they struggle, and how future AI assistance might support more effective training.
